## Recommended use

- Use **only** for B2B outreach where the recipient has a public business
  phone number listed for inbound contact (Google My Business, public
  directory).
- Use the slowest delays you can tolerate — `45–90 seconds` minimum between
  messages.
- Send small batches (`<= 30/day` per number) and stop on the first complaint.
- **Never** harvest personal phone numbers from non-business sources.
